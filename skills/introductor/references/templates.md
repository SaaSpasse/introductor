# Templates - warm email introductions

## Template 1 - Standard two-way intro (casual)

```
Subject: Intro: Sarah ↔ Mike - AI hiring tools

Hey Sarah,

Je te connecte avec Mike Chen - il est CTO chez TalentAI et ils ont buildé un outil de screening qui a coupé le temps d'embauche de 40% chez leurs clients. Il cherche des early adopters dans le SaaS B2B. Son site: talentai.com

Mike, Sarah dirige le recrutement chez Acme (50+ hires cette année). Elle m'a parlé la semaine dernière de ses frustrations avec le volume de CVs. Je pense que vous avez des choses à vous dire.

Je vous laisse jaser - bougez-moi en BCC!
```

## Structure patterns

### The nugget formula

For each person, write ONE sentence that answers: "Why should the other person care?"

**Good nuggets:**
- "She reduced churn by 40% at a 500-person SaaS" (specific, impressive)
- "He sold his Shopify app for $5M in 24 months" (concrete, credible)
- "She's been building dev tools for 10 years and just raised a Series B" (experience + milestone)

**Bad nuggets:**
- "He's a great guy" (says nothing)
- "She has 15 years of experience in the industry" (generic)
- "He's the CEO of a successful company" (vague)

### The link formula

For each person, include ONE link that lets the recipient learn more:

**Best links by context:**
- Podcast appearance → episode page (shows they have stories worth telling)
- Company → website (shows what they've built)
- Recent press → article (shows momentum)
- LinkedIn → profile (generic but always works)

**Don't link to:**
- Multiple URLs for the same person
- Social media that isn't relevant
- Generic company "about" pages
